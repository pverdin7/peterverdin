01 FIND THE ANGLE

See the opportunity.
Shape the idea.

A strong idea starts with understanding the opportunity—and seeing an angle worth pursuing.

THE CHALLENGE

Connect audience insight and market opportunity to a persuasive, client-facing idea.

MY CONTRIBUTION

I developed research-led proposals and campaign concepts for a coffee bar, a university, live entertainment, and Terry Reilly Health Services. Each audience called for a different way to make the case.

See how the thinking became work +

A demographic brief for Slow by Slow Coffee Bar. A proposal for Northwest Nazarene University. A Live Nation / Trans-Siberian Orchestra campaign concept. And For a Reason, a proposed television campaign for Terry Reilly Health Services.

Each paired research with a specific creative message. The NNU proposal resulted in a closed deal; the Live Nation pitch was a winning proposal.

↓Sometimes the opportunity is in the audience.

02 CONNECT THE DISCIPLINES

Look wider.
Find a better way.

Research, learning, and visual design can come together to solve a bigger problem.

THE CHALLENGE

Translate long-term athletic development and physical literacy into a physical-education curriculum.

MY CONTRIBUTION

At Athlos, I brought writing, research, and visual design into the same process—building and refining lessons, then designing their pages in InDesign with graphics in Illustrator.

I independently wrote the 448 K–5 lessons, collaborated on 150 lessons for grades 8–9, and revised 153 lessons for grades 6–7. Together, the curriculum contains more than 750 lessons.

The work extended from the learning model to visual teaching resources and sample plans.

It can also open up an entirely new possibility.

DOCTORAL RESEARCH / MOTOR LEARNING

Ask a more
useful question.

My doctoral research examined voice inflection and an external focus of attention during a standing long jump in fourth- and fifth-grade students.

A closer look at how the way we give an instruction relates to the experience of learning.

CUSTOMER SUCCESS / ONBOARDING

the person using it.
